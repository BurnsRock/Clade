# CLADE v0.1 · Rules and Scope

- You play a lineage, not an individual creature.
- Three lethal enemy encounters, each in a distinct environment, form a complete three-generation run.
- A ten-card genome powers combat; the innate reproductive action sits outside that deck.
- Preparation (turn 0) prevents attacking but allows defensive setup and reproduction.
- Combat starts with 3 Energy per turn. Playing gene cards triggers their effects; enemies telegraph their next action.
- Environment effects are separately telegraphed and can damage both sides.
- You must defeat the enemy **and** produce at least one offspring or the lineage ends.
- Both reproduction actions are innate, once per turn, and can produce up to three offspring candidates per generation.
- Asexual Cell Division costs 1 Energy, copies the genome, and has a 12% spontaneous mutation chance.
- Sexual Mating costs 2 Energy and recombines two genes with a simplified compatible-partner pool.
- Error-Prone Replication costs 1 Energy and 2 HP to guarantee one extra mutation in the next offspring.
- After combat, choose your successor's genome and optionally inherit a reward gene.
- Reinforced Membrane requires Cell Membrane; Neural Complexity requires Neural Signaling; Protect Offspring requires sexual reproduction and both cognition genes.
- No full speciation, alleles, relic inventory, epigenetic inheritance, mate simulation, animations, or audio in this first slice.

**Design priority:** a fun deckbuilder first, science-inspired rules second. Seeded immutable simulation transitions make future balancing and testing easier.
